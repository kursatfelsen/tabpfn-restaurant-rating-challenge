      How it works

      Each card is a real restaurant listing from the Zomato Bangalore dataset. You see what a diner would see: votes, price, cuisine, popular dishes and customer reviews. The overall rating is hidden.

      You play against three models: TabPFN, XGBoost and Linear Regression. Each made its predictions before you arrived. They were fitted on the same 37,384 other listings and never saw any of the restaurants in this game. The setup follows the Prior Labs cookbook Predict Restaurant Ratings with TabPFN 3.5.

      This is a regression task, so everyone is scored on error, where lower is better. MAE is the average distance from the real rating. RMSE is the root mean squared error, which punishes big misses more. Each round you beat every model whose prediction is further from the real rating than your guess.

        The map is decorative. Streets and neighbours are generated, not real locations.
